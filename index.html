1. Click anywhere inside the dashed box below, then press Ctrl + A (Windows) or Cmd + A (Mac) to select the signature.
  2. Press Ctrl + C / Cmd + C to copy.
  3. In Gmail, open the gear icon → See all settings.
  4. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Give it a name.
  5. Click into the big empty signature box and press Ctrl + V / Cmd + V to paste.
  6. Under Signature defaults, choose your new signature for both dropdowns.
  7. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Please copy from this page, not from the Word or Google document. Copying from a document pastes flat pictures and the links stop working. Copying from here keeps every link clickable.
